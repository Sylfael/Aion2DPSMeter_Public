# Aion2DPSMeter 0.8.1

New in 0.8.1:
- The Party tab now shows exactly your current party. When you left a dungeon room as a member, the people of that run used to stay in the list and show up next to your next group (for example six names while the game showed four). Now the tab lists only the people in the game's latest room or party list, and someone who leaves or is kicked disappears at once.
- If the meter starts while you are already in a party, the Party tab says so and tells you when the full list will arrive: the game sends it when you enter a dungeon or another map server, and when someone joins or leaves.
- Dungeon names in the inspector header, for example "Shattered Arkanis · Stage 4" instead of "dungeon 610062". A dungeon the meter does not know yet still shows its number.
- Pets window: every card at the Black Cloud merchant now shows which pet it is for, and gets a real verdict (unlocks, levels up, useful or wasted) instead of "unknown card".

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 and Npcap.
