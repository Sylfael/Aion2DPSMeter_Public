# Aion2DPSMeter 0.11.0

An alerts bell in the status bar, and windows that stay where you put them.

- **Active alerts.** A small bell with a number appears in the status bar while something needs your attention: a field boss you follow is coming or is up, a character's Odyle is full or its Nightmare tickets are at the cap, an update is out, or the game protocol is not verified yet. Its colour shows the most urgent one. Hover or click it to see the list; click a line to open the right window (the World window on that boss, the Tickets window on that character, or Settings), or ✕ to hide it until it is over. It stays visible when the meter is a slim strip or hidden. Turn it off, or choose what it lists, in Settings → Alerts. Muted characters never show. The pop-up toasts work as before.
- **The meter comes back where you left it.** Its place and width are restored reliably at every start, before it appears, on the same monitor, also in "Grow upwards" mode. If that monitor is gone, it goes to the top left of your main screen.
- **Every window remembers its place.** The applicants, fight details, World, Pets, Tickets and Settings windows now open where you left them, at the same size (maximised windows open maximised when you open them yourself). A window whose screen is gone opens centred.
- **Reset window positions** in Settings → General puts every window back to its default place.
