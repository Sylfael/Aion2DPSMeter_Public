# Aion2DPSMeter 0.10.0

A design pass over every window.

- **Settings window.** All settings now live in their own window with tabs: General, Appearance, Meter, Party, Alerts, Capture and About. Every option has a short explanation. Open it from the tray (Settings…) or from "All settings…" in the overlay. Changes apply to the overlay at once.
- **Quick panel.** The overlay's ⚙ now holds only what you change mid-fight: size, boss fights only, idle mode, hide in game menus and click-through.
- **Overlay.** Numbers on the class bars are easier to read, column headers are aligned, alert toasts no longer push the rows down, and the click-through hint no longer covers the boss name. Footer buttons are larger, and text never gets smaller than 9 px at 85 %.
- **Ping colour** compares with your own best ping of the session, so a stable ExitLag ping is no longer always amber.
- **All windows.** One style for buttons, tables and empty states; drawn icons instead of text symbols; visible keyboard focus. Fight details group the summary into output, activity, hit quality and defence.
