# Aion2DPSMeter 0.22.1

Four small fixes on top of 0.22.0. Nothing else changed.

- **Fight details keeps the fight you picked.** Clicking the personal-best notice, "Open Records" in Settings, or Fight details in the tray menu no longer jumps back to the overlay's fight. The Fight details button on the overlay still shows the overlay's fight.
- **Tracker stays on the screen.** When Tracker takes over the place of your old World window (the first start after 0.21.0), it is now kept inside the screen, also on a laptop at 150 % scale.
- **A clearer notice when a part of the meter keeps failing.** It now says the part is failing repeatedly and still trying, and the notice goes away when it works again. Before, it said "stopped" and stayed until you quit.
- **From the update after this one on:** if you start the meter yourself while an update is finishing, you no longer get an "already running" message.
